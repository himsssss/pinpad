Pinpad Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Pinpad is a board app for notes, links, images and to-dos, available as a website and as apps for macOS and Windows. Pinpad keeps as little about you as it can: your boards stay wherever you save them, and we never sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it beyond the services named below.

Where your boards are saved

Your boards are saved only in the place you choose in Storage location:

  • This browser (website): in your browser's own storage on your device.
  • This computer (Mac and Windows apps): as files in a folder you choose.
  • Google Drive: as files in a Pinpad folder in your own Google Drive.

Nobody else, including the makers of Pinpad, can see your boards.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, Pinpad asks Google for the drive.file permission only. This lets Pinpad create files in your Drive and read and change those same files. It can't see any other file in your Drive. Pinpad also reads your Google account's email address so it can show which account is connected.

Pinpad uses this access only to save and open your boards. The data goes directly between your device and Google; it never passes through a Pinpad server. The sign-in token stays on your device: for the browser session on the website, or in your computer's secure storage (Keychain on macOS) in the apps. Disconnecting in Storage location signs Pinpad out. You can also remove Pinpad's access at any time from your Google Account.

Pinpad's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Pinpad doesn't use this data for advertising, doesn't sell it, doesn't let people read it, and doesn't use it to train AI models.

Your Pinpad account

You sign in to Pinpad with an email address and a password. Accounts are run on Google's Firebase: Firebase Authentication keeps your email address and a protected (hashed) form of your password, and sends the emails that confirm your address or reset your password. Your account doesn't hold your boards. It only remembers which Google Drive your boards are in (that Google account's email address) and when that was last updated, so signing in on another computer can find them. This is kept in Google Cloud Firestore, in India (Mumbai), and only you can read it while you're signed in.

Your sign-in stays on your device (in the browser's storage on the website, or in the app's storage) until you sign out. To delete your account and what it remembers, email the address at the bottom of this page.

Links and embedded media

When you add a link, Pinpad reads the start of that page to show its title, picture and description. In the Mac and Windows apps, your computer reads the page itself. On the website, browsers can't read other sites' pages, so the link is sent to Pinpad's link reader (hosted with the website on Vercel). It fetches the page, sends the start of it back to you and keeps nothing. To save repeating the work, Vercel can reuse the answer for the same link for up to a day.

Videos and audio you add (for example from YouTube, Vimeo, Spotify or SoundCloud) play from the site that hosts them. That site, and any site whose page is read for a link, handles the request under its own privacy policy.

Error reports

When something goes wrong in Pinpad, it sends an error report to Sentry so the problem can be found and fixed. A report contains the error message, where in Pinpad's code it happened, your browser or app version and operating system, the address of the Pinpad page and of the requests it made just before the error (without anything after a ?). It doesn't include your boards, what you typed or clicked, your name or email address, your account's id, cookies, or sign-in tokens. Sentry uses the internet (IP) address a report comes from to note the country, and doesn't keep the address itself.

Hosting

The website is hosted on Vercel, which keeps routine request logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) for a short time to run and protect the site.

Deleting your data

Delete boards in Pinpad, or delete the files directly: clear the site's data in your browser, delete the storage folder on your computer, or delete the Pinpad folder in Google Drive. To delete your Pinpad account, email us and we'll remove it and what it remembers.
